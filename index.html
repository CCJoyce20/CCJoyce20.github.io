Calvin Joyce

M.S. Mechanical Engineering @ UC San Diego ARCLab · Medical Robotics · Haptics · Robot Learning

Advanced Robotics and Controls Lab

UC San Diego, La Jolla, CA

I build robots that physically interact with people, and the hard part isn’t the motion, it’s the contact.

At UC San Diego’s ARCLab (advisor: Prof. Michael Yip), I lead PHABS, a handheld bimanual haptic device capturing force-annotated demonstrations for imitation learning, and RIC, the first framework for robots that physically handle infants. I’m a co-author on the first Nature study of humanoid robots in surgery, and my redesigned camera mount fields a Phase II neonatal monitoring trial at Mount Sinai.

I started in mechanical design and now work up the stack: autonomy, controls, and robot learning, deployed on real hardware.

Seeking medical robotics R&D / New Product Development internships, January–September 2027.
