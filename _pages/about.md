I build robots that physically interact with people, and I've spent the last four years learning that the hard part isn't the motion, it's the contact.

At UC San Diego's [ARCLab](https://ucsdarclab.com) (advisor: Prof. Michael Yip), I lead two projects headed to ICRA 2027: **PHABS**, a handheld bimanual haptic device that captures force-annotated demonstrations for imitation learning, and **RIC**, the first framework for robots that physically handle infants, built with NICU clinicians around measurable safety thresholds. I'm also a co-author on the first [*Nature* study of humanoid robots in surgery](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10796-x), and at ArtemisAI Labs my redesigned camera mount is running in a Phase II neonatal monitoring trial at Mount Sinai.

I started in mechanical design: CAD, FEA, capstan drives, full device builds. That hardware depth is now the foundation for my work in autonomy, controls, and robot learning. I like owning the whole loop: design the hardware, close the control loop, train the policy, validate against a clinical bar.

Currently seeking **medical robotics R&D / New Product Development internships, January–September 2027**.
