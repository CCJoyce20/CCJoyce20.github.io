At ArtemisAI Labs I redesigned a NICU bedside camera mount for real clinical deployment: stability, theft resistance, IV-pole grip, and full screen access, built from low-cost, manufacturable, and COTS components.

That mount now fields the cameras for **NOVA (Neonatal AI Vision Assessment)**, ArtemisAI's Phase II multi-center clinical trial of computer-vision neurological monitoring of newborns, running at Mount Sinai. I'm also lining up assembly partners to kit and ship units to hospitals purchasing the device.
